# Full-band production reproduction

This directory distinguishes the preserved historical data, the first corrected
recalculation, and an independent repeat against the current paper data.

- `archived_reference/`: all 12 historical CSV/JSON inputs, preserved byte for
  byte before promotion. They are also covered by the untouched recovery snapshot.
- `historical_comparison/`: the complete corrected recalculation and its original
  comparison report. Its `status` remains `FAIL`; the difference is not concealed.
- `reproduction_metadata.json`, `comparison.csv`, `comparison_cells.csv`,
  `convergence_raw.csv`, and `data_prl/full_bz_completion/`: an independent repeat
  against the promoted, current paper data. Read the report status for the result.
- `fresh_validation.json` and `fresh_validation.log`: the existing full-band
  scientific validator executed against the independently regenerated tables.

The unchanged production generators calculate the Lx=22, Nky=64, Nkz=36 slab,
the L=6 and L=8 magnetic tori at flux -1, 0, +1, and the seeded Landau projection.
The slab fields are B=(0.0008, 0.0012, 0.0016, 0.0020). No stored tables are
copied into the calculation sandbox before these generators execute.

The historical convergence aggregator was not recovered. The current runner
documents its replacement: evaluate all four even-field quotients, fit a quadratic
polynomial in B^2, retain its B=0 intercept, and apply the fixed-density chain rule
using a central chemical-potential derivative with step 2e-5. All 7 transverse-grid
rows and 3 slab-width rows are regenerated (9 distinct grids, with one shared row).
The intermediate 36 field/grid rows are retained in `convergence_raw.csv`.
The real-response convergence diagnostic compares the real fitted coefficient
with the real chain-rule coefficient; the production summary separately keeps
the complete complex response and its complex chain-rule diagnostic.

The historical Nky=16 real chain-rule residual was 3.795701499195574e-6.
The reconstructed value is about 1.64832e-6: their difference, 2.14738e-6,
exceeds the predeclared 1e-6 absolute diagnostic-comparison tolerance. Accordingly,
the historical comparison remains a failure for that one diagnostic cell.
This does not recover or certify the missing historical aggregation recipe.
The current active tables were instead generated by the documented replacement
and then independently recomputed with that same openly inspectable recipe.

The Landau example has a numerical 40-dimensional zero-eigenvalue subspace.
Individual squared overlaps of its eigenvectors are basis dependent: all 80 raw
charge/axial overlap differences remain visible in the historical cell report.
The physical comparison requires every eigenvalue, every isolated-mode overlap,
and the summed charge/axial projector weight in each degenerate eigenspace.
For an orthonormal eigenspace basis U and probe v, the latter is ||U^dagger v||^2,
which is unchanged by U -> U W for unitary W. Clusters use the documented numerical
resolution 1e-12 * max(1, spectral radius), not a recovered choice of eigenvectors.
The archived-to-fresh zero-subspace total weights differ by less than 3e-17;
the leading 8 overlaps and all 48 eigenvalues also agree to numerical precision.

Per-cell absolute and relative differences, the actual allowed error, and the
reason for the comparison are recorded. Machine-roundoff Ward and gauge residuals
use absolute bounds; matching their relative roundoff fluctuations is not a
physical reproducibility requirement. No tolerance was widened to erase the
historical diagnostic discrepancy. Degenerate raw overlaps are retained with
`required_for_scientific_match=False` and checked through projector weights instead.

To repeat, use a new, nonexistent output folder outside the paper source tree:

    python code/full_bz_completion/reproduce_production.py --output-root H:\Research\.paper-runtime\full-band\new-run

Set TMP, TEMP, and MPLCONFIGDIR to H-drive locations before launching on the
current workstation; set PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 and OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS,
OMP_NUM_THREADS, MKL_NUM_THREADS to 1. The runner refuses to overwrite an existing
output folder, records versions and source/reference/generated SHA256 hashes,
and checks that its sources and reference tables remain unchanged during the run.
The preserved report paths refer to the original isolated runtime location;
their generated hashes resolve relative to each copied report directory here.

These are full-band response, fixed-density, Ward-identity and Landau-projection
benchmarks. They do not supply a single interacting lattice calculation of the
charge-axial avoided-crossing gap, and they do not establish a thermodynamic-limit
or material-specific result.
